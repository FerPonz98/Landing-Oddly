All artwork was supplied in this repository by the user. No logo was generated or redrawn.

- `oddly-dark.webp`: `public/logo/LOGO _ODDLY_AZUL_OBSCURO_.png`
- `oddly-light.webp`: `public/logo/LOGO_ODDLY_BLANCO.png`
- `oddly-orange.webp`: `public/logo/LOGO_ODDLY_NARANJA.png`
- `oddly-symbol.webp`, `src/app/icon.png`, `src/app/apple-icon.png`: `public/logo/logo-03.png`
- `src/app/opengraph-image.png`: `public/logo/LOGO _ODDLY_AZUL_OBSCURO_.png`

`scripts/prepare-brand-assets.mjs` crops transparent outer margins and resizes the supplied art. App icons and the social preview composite it on the landing's light background.
